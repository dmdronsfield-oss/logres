inkxo-knight.glb is the complete model and rig, unaltered except that its textures were
reduced to 512 px WebP and the file is Draco-compressed for phones. In Logres it plays
Sir Gawain; the game closes its gauntlets into fists and drives its skeleton with
Logres's combat motion. The sword and lion shield in his hands come from gawain.glb.

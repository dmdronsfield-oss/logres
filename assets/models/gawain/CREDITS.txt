Sir Gawain (gawain.glb / gawain.blend) for Logres.

Armour: derived from "Medieval Knight Armor (Low-poly / Rigged)" by INKXO,
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/medieval-knight-armor-low-poly-rigged-428913948904425e8daec81eea9aa287
licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Changes: refitted to Logres's 23-bone skeleton and body proportions; gauntlets closed
into fists; reduced from ~100k to ~10k flat-shaded triangles with colours sampled from
INKXO's textures; plume recoloured blue; the sword drawn from the scabbard to be the
weapon in hand.

Skeleton, heater shield and lion device: made for Logres.

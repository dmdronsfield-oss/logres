# Brief 11: they fight back

An opening is currently free damage until it times out. Better foes should make you choose when to
stop.

## 1. Each foe's limit
- **Saxon, Black Knight:** no limit; they're the teachers.
- **Brigand:** after 3 cuts in one opening he hops back out of reach, ending it.
- **Red Knight:** after 3 cuts he turns your 4th aside and answers with a quick counter-cut.
- **Green Knight:** in his second phase, the same after 4 cuts.
Staggers and the super ignore these limits: a staggered man can't defend himself.

## 2. Read the opening closing
The gold "open" shimmer fades as the opening runs out, and flickers just before a foe with a limit
recovers (on the cut before his limit), so you can stop in time.

## 3. Teach it once
"He won't stand for it: stop before he recovers."

## Done when
Each limit triggers correctly (bot-checked); the warning flicker reads; release published.

# Brief 9: weapons with weight

Every foe swings the same library of blows. The weapon should change how a blow moves, so the eye
learns each foe's rhythm.

## 1. Swing styles by weapon
- **Heavy** (the Saxon's axe, the Green Knight's greataxe): a deeper coil, a bigger lean, a long
  follow-through that drags the body round, and a slower settle afterwards.
- **Quick** (the Brigand's falchion): a short coil and a snapping recovery, hands close to the body.
- **Formal** (the knights' swords): as now.

## 2. He blocks you, visibly
When your swipe clangs off his guard, he visibly takes it: on his shield if he carries one (raised and
angled into the blow), otherwise on his blade, crosswise.

## Done when
The three styles are visibly different in the lab and in duels; the shield and blade blocks read; the
bot passes; release published.

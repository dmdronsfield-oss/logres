foe's chest. It covers the body you're cutting, the gold open glow, and the reaction to your blows.
The callouts ("Disarmed!", "Parry!") also land on the label.

  little see-through, so his body and the glow read through it.
- Move the label off his body to the side, so the text never covers him or the callouts.
- Check both layouts (landscape and portrait) with each foe's size, including the giant Green Knight.

release published.

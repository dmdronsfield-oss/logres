# Brief 14: the Pendragon's strike, and Gawain's arsenal

## 1. Strike of the Pendragon (the super)
It's currently a flash and a stun. Make it the best moment you can trigger yourself:
- Slow motion; the camera drops low and looks up at Gawain.
- He raises his sword overhead; the blade blazes gold and lights the hall; then he brings it down.
- A shock of gold light; the foe is thrown back, stunned. The crowd roars.

## 2. The arsenal you earn, visible
Unlocks change only numbers today; Gawain always holds the same sword and lion shield. Now:
- **Squire's Sword:** plain steel. **Clarent:** gilded, with a warm sheen. **Excalibur:** the blade
  glows with a pale light, and its trail is brighter.
- **Ash Targe:** a round wooden targe. **Kite of Logres:** Gawain's own lion heater. **Pridwen:** a
  heater of blue with gold crowns and a soft glow.

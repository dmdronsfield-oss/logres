# Brief 3: worthy opponents

Gawain is now a detailed, textured knight, but the knights he fights are still the simple code-built
figures, and that's what you watch all fight long. Fix the mismatch for the armoured foes.

## Work
- **Black Knight and Red Knight wear INKXO's harness** (the same CC BY model Gawain uses), recoloured:
  the Black Knight in blackened steel, the Red Knight in red lacquer with gilt edges.
- **Their own arms:** they carry their own weapons and their own heraldic shields (the code-built ones
  with their charges), not Gawain's lion.
- **Same motion:** they're driven by the same retargeting as Gawain, so every blow, parry reaction,
  stagger and the finishing blow work unchanged.
- **Keep it fast on phones:** check draw calls and frame time with two detailed knights on screen. If
  needed, drop the second figure's shadows or texture size.
- The Saxon, the Brigand and the Green Knight stay as they are; they aren't plate knights, and the
  contrast suits them.

## Done when
Both knights look right from the game camera and in portrait; a bot completes their duels; the
performance check is noted; release published.

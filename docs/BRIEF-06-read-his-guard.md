# Brief 6: read his guard

Between his attacks there's nothing to do but wait, and swiping only clangs. Give the patient,
observant player a way in.

## 1. High guard / low guard
While he's waiting, he holds either a **high guard** (blade raised) or a **low guard** (point low), and
shifts between them every couple of seconds.
- A high guard leaves the body open to a **horizontal cut** (← or →).
- A low guard leaves him open to a **vertical cut** (↓ or ↑).
- A cut through the gap gets in for half damage ("Through his guard!"). He adapts at once (changes
  guard) and comes at you sooner. Anything else clangs, and two clangs still earn his counter-cut.
- A faint arrow over him shows the gap, quieter than the gold "open" arrows. Taught once.
- The Green Knight's axe has no such guard; he stays as he is.

## 2. Gawain's own moves, more legible
- **Dodge:** a real evasion (a crouched side-step with the shoulders turning away), not just a slide.
- **Block:** the shield braced with the whole body; a jolt and a step back on impact.
- **Struck:** a heavier stagger back, the head snapping with the blow's direction.

# Brief 19: the last two code-built foes

The Saxon and the Brigand were still the simple figures from the first 3D test, beside four detailed
knights. Rather than borrow plate for men who wouldn't wear it, use INKXO's model selectively.

## What was found
Measuring INKXO's materials in Blender: the helmet is six materials that sit only on the head (steel,
edge rolls, rims, lining, rivets, plume), and under the plate there's a full mail hauberk with sleeves,
plus gloves and boots. Stripped of plate, it's a 6th-century byrnie.

## Built
- **Hide by material:** a foe's look can now hide whole materials of the borrowed model, recolour others
  (plate legs as leather, a breastplate as a brigandine), and carry a code-built head and helm on the
  head bone.
- **The Saxon:** mail hauberk, leather leggings, his own nasal helm and red beard, round shield, axe.
- **The Brigand:** mail sleeves, a dark leather brigandine, leather legs, his green hood, buckler,
  falchion.
- Everyone in the hall now shares one body and its motion.

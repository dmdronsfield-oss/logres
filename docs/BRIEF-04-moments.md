# Brief 4: moments

Duels need peaks, not just a steady rhythm. Three additions.

## 1. Blade lock
Parry at the very last instant (in the final 70 ms before impact) and the blades bind instead of
glancing off. The camera tightens and both knights lean in, blades crossed, sparks grinding. Tap fast
(anywhere, or any key) to drive him back before he forces you down.
- Win: he's thrown off balance ("Overpowered!") and staggered.
- Lose: he shoves through and cuts you, lightly.
It reads at a glance: a gold strength bar between the two names, and the knights' distance shifts
with it. It can't happen in the tutorial. It's taught once.

## 2. Where it came from
When a blow lands on you, the edge of the screen it came from flashes red, so even a hit you missed
teaches you its direction.

## 3. Last stand
Every foe without a scripted second phase gets one at 30% health: a roar, a short banner ("…fights on,
desperate!"), slightly faster wind-ups and shorter pauses. The Green Knight keeps his own.

## Done when
The lock plays in landscape and portrait, from touch and keyboard; the bot completes all duels
(winning and losing locks); release published.

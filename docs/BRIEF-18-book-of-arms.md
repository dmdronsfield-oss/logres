# Brief 18: the Book of Arms

The duel now has around fifteen techniques and foe tricks. They're taught by one-off banners, which
are easy to miss and can't be looked up. Add a reference in camp.

## The book
- A **Book of Arms** button in camp opens an illuminated page (parchment, gold capitals) listing:
  - **Your arts:** strike, dodge, block, parry, riposte, perfect block, counter, known strikes, the
    gap in his guard, blade lock, Strike of the Pendragon.
  - **Their tricks:** red glints, thrusts, the shield bash, the hesitation, the counter-cut,
    recovering early, the last stand.
- Each entry gives what it is, how to do or answer it (touch and keyboard), and why it matters.
- Entries you haven't met yet show as sealed ("Not yet seen"), so the book fills as you play. The basics are always open.
- Works upright and sideways; scrolls by drag, wheel or arrow keys; Esc or the button closes it.

## Done when
The book opens from camp in both orientations; entries unlock from the lesson flags; release published.

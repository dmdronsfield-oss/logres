# Brief 2: smarter, more distinct foes

Follows the overnight brief. Goal: every foe should feel like a different fight, and the game should
reward reading him over mashing.

## 1. Punish swipe-spam
Swiping at a guarded foe already clangs. Now, two clangs within 1.2 s and he answers at once: a quick
counter-cut (short wind-up, "His counter!" in red). It stays parryable and dodgeable, so it's a lesson,
not a trap.

## 2. A signature move per foe
- **Saxon raider: shield bash.** He drives his round shield at you. It can't be parried or blocked
  (it knocks your shield aside); only a dodge works. The ring is red, with a "DODGE" tag the first few times.
- **Black Knight: hesitation strike.** An overhead that stops at the top of the wind-up before it falls.
  The approach ring visibly pauses too, so the patient player still reads it.
- The Brigand (flurries), Red Knight (feints, punishes early dodges, the reap) and Green Knight
  (unparryable sweeps, second phase) already have theirs.

## 3. Readability for the new moves
Each new move gets a distinct pose in 3D, a label under the ring the first times you see it, and a
one-time explanation.

## Done when
A bot completes all five duels with no errors; the bash and hesitation read clearly in screenshots;
release published with notes.

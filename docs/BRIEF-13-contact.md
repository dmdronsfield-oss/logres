# Brief 13: contact

A hit should look like steel meeting a body at a place, not a flash at his chest.

## 1. Sparks where the blade lands
Each of your hits throws its sparks from where your blade actually crosses him on screen, sprayed
along the direction of the cut.

## 2. Where you hit him matters to how he moves
- High cuts (↓, ↑ and the diagonals from above) snap his head back and to the side.
- Level cuts (← →) twist his trunk round.
- The head gets its own spring, separate from the trunk.

## 3. Battle wear
As a fight goes on, the armour shows it: at two-thirds and one-third health a knight's plate darkens
and dulls a step (dirt, dents, lost polish). Gawain too, as he's hurt.

## Done when
Sparks sit on the blade line in screenshots; head snaps read; wear is visible but subtle; the bot
passes; release published.

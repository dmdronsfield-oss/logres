# Brief 22: voices and steel

Sound carries timing and weight as much as sight does, and the foes are mute.

## Work (all synthesised; still no audio files)
- **Battle cries:** a short shout as each wind-up begins (a second timing cue, alongside the glint
  and the whoosh), with louder ones for heavy and unparryable blows. Each foe has his own voice:
  the Saxon gruff, the Black Knight muffled in his helm, the Brigand sharp, the Red Knight ringing,
  the Green Knight deep.
- **Pain:** a grunt when you cut him (a bigger one when staggered), and Gawain grunts when hit.
- **What you hit:** plate rings, mail chinks, leather thuds. Each foe's hits sound like his armour.
- **Footfalls:** heavy steps on his lunges and yours; a scuff on dodges.

## Done when
Each sound plays at its moment without clutter; voices differ by foe; the bot passes (no errors);
release published.

The final foe was still the simple code-built figure. Give him the same detailed harness as the other
knights, in green and gold, and keep what makes him himself.

- INKXO's harness (CC BY) tinted green with gold edges and a green plume, at 1.2× Gawain's size.
- His antler crown, now set on the borrowed helm (antlers split out of the code-built helmet so any
  model can wear them).

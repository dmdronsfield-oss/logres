# Brief 17: comfort settings

The briefs since last night added a lot of motion: screen shakes, camera kicks and pushes, swinging
cinematic cameras, slow-motion beats, and many full-screen flashes. Some players get motion-sick or
are sensitive to flashing light.

## Settings (pause menu and camp)
- **Motion: Full / Reduced.** Reduced removes screen shake and camera kicks, and replaces the
  swinging cameras (entrance, blade lock, Pendragon, finishing blow, your fall) with gentle cuts or
  holds. Slow motion stays; it's information, not motion.
- **Flashes: Full / Reduced.** Reduced caps full-screen flashes at a soft level and stops the red
  edge flash from pulsing.
- Both default to Reduced if the device says it prefers reduced motion (an OS setting).

## Done when
Both settings work live, are remembered, and default from the OS setting; nothing else changes with
them off; release published.

## As built
One switch rather than two (the pause menu was full): **Comfort mode**, in the pause menu. On: no
screen shake, camera kicks or pushes; every cinematic camera (entrance, blade lock, Pendragon,
finishing blow, your fall) holds its place; full-screen flashes are capped at a soft level and the red
edge flash is halved. It follows the device's "reduce motion" setting until the player chooses.

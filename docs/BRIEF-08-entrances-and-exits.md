# Brief 8: entrances and exits

A duel should begin like a challenge and end like a fall. Victory already has its finishing blow;
the start and your defeat are flat.

## 1. The challenge
- **Camera:** each duel opens on a wide, high view of the hall that sweeps down into the
  over-the-shoulder view while he walks in.
- **His challenge**, one per foe, struck as he arrives:
  - Saxon: beats his axe on his shield.
  - Black Knight: a formal salute, hilt to visor.
  - Brigand: points his falchion at you.
  - Red Knight: raises his sword high.
  - Green Knight: spreads his arms and laughs.
- The crowd stirs, and the name card holds through the entrance. You can't be attacked until he's
  in guard.

## 2. The fall
When you're beaten: slow motion, the HUD clears, the camera swings round to face Gawain as he goes
down, the crowd gasps and the music drops away, then the chapel.

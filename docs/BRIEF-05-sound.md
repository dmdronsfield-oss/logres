# Brief 5: the sound of the hall

The fights look dramatic now, but they sound thin. Make the hall sound alive and let the music carry
the tension. Everything stays synthesised in code (no audio files).

- **Music:** a bourdon drone, a plucked harp improvising in D Dorian, and a tabor that goes from a
  heartbeat to a war-march. Moods: camp (title), duel, stand (last stand and blade lock, faster and
  denser), victory (a horn call), silence (defeat). A Music on/off setting in camp and the pause menu.
- **The crowd:** a murmur under every duel; a cheer for parries, won locks and last stands; a gasp
  when you're struck.
- **Weightier steel:** parries ring with inharmonic partials, a hard transient and a body thud;
  clangs knock heavily and vary in pitch.
- **Low health:** your heartbeat, quickening as you weaken.

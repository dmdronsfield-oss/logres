# Brief 20: pride and life

## 1. Life between blows
While waiting, a foe shouldn't stand like a statue: he shifts his weight from foot to foot, resets
his feet now and then with a small step, and his breathing shows in the shoulders. Each foe at his
own rhythm (the Brigand restless, the Black Knight still, the Green Knight slow and heavy).

## 2. Pride comes before a fall
If he lands two blows on you in a row, he gloats: his own challenge pose (the Saxon beats his shield,
the Red Knight raises his sword…) for about a second. While he gloats he's OPEN ("He gloats!
Strike!"), and the crowd jeers. It's a comeback for a player who's being beaten, and it shows his
character. Once per three of his attacks at most.

## Done when
Idle life reads in the lab; the gloat triggers after two hits, is open, and is taught once; the bot
passes; release published.
